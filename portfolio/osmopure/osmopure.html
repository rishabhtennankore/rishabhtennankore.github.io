Overview

OsmoPure Technologies is currently developing and validating a pilot-scale membrane platform aimed at commercializing pressure-distillation processes.

My contributions focused on building an execution-ready R&D loop: define a reliable test workflow, iterate through coating formulations and architectures guided by DOE, and connect morphology/surface properties to transport performance - towards the scalable manufacture of these membranes.
