Overview

OsmoPure is developing pressure-driven distillation membranes for high-value water purification and reuse. Pressure distillation leverages hydrophobic porous media to pass vapor while rejecting dissolved species.

My contributions focused on building an execution-ready R&D loop: define a reliable test workflow, iterate coating architectures with DOE, and connect morphology/surface properties to transport performance.
