Overview

At OsmoPure Technologies, I helped build the membrane R&D program from the ground up—defining test workflows, selecting materials, running DOE-guided iteration, and connecting coating morphology/surface properties to separation performance.

The work focused on scalable silicone and sol–gel coating architectures for hydrophobic modification of porous substrates, with rapid feedback from characterization and performance benchmarking to guide design decisions and scale-up priorities.
