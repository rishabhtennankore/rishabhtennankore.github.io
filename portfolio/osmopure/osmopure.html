Overview

Osmopure is developing membranes for pressure-driven purification where a thin hydrophobic top layer must sustain high liquid entry pressures without sacrificing transport. My contribution focused on establishing quantitative coating control, resolving substrate/process failure modes, and developing a multilayer architecture that significantly increased pressure stability and enabled measurable salt rejection.

Note: This case study is intentionally non-proprietary and focuses on approach, physical reasoning, and validation strategy.

What I Did

Built thickness metrology + process calibration for blade-coated sol-gel films; resolved substrate/solvent incompatibility; introduced pore backfilling to limit wicking; diagnosed pressure-failure as heterogeneity-driven global breakthrough; and validated a multilayer membrane architecture that improved pressure tolerance and enabled measurable salt rejection.
